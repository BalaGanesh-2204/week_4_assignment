# Account and Security Help

Everything about creating, managing, and securing your ShopKart account.

## Creating an Account

You can create an account with an email address and password, or continue with Google.

Accounts are free and required for order tracking, returns, and saved addresses.

Guest checkout is available but orders placed as guest cannot be tracked in the app.

## Updating Profile Information

You can change your name, phone number, and addresses from Account Settings.

Our support agent can also save your preferences (for example preferred contact method) so you do not have to repeat them every visit.

## Password Reset

Use Forgot Password on the sign-in page.

Reset links expire after 30 minutes.

If the email does not arrive, check spam, then wait 10 minutes before requesting again.

## Two-Factor Authentication

Two-factor authentication (2FA) is available for all accounts and strongly recommended.

With 2FA enabled, signing in from a new device requires a code sent to your registered email or authenticator app.

## Suspicious Activity

If you notice an order you did not place:

1. Change your password immediately.
2. Contact support and flag the order.
3. We freeze pending shipments on suspicious orders while we investigate.

Confirmed unauthorized transactions are refunded in full and escalated to our security team.

## Data and Privacy

You can request a copy of your data or account deletion from privacy settings.

Deletion removes personal data but keeps anonymized financial records as required by law for 7 years.

## Agent Limitations

For your safety, the support agent will never:

- Ask for your password or full card details.
- Change your email address on file.
- Process account deletions.

These require identity verification with our human team, and the agent can escalate for you.
