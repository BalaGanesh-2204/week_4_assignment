# Payments, Billing and Promotions FAQ

Answers about paying, invoices, promo codes and store credit.

## Accepted Payment Methods

- Credit and debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, Amex).
- UPI and wallet payments.
- Net banking.
- ShopKart gift cards.
- Cash on delivery for orders under $300 in metro and urban zones.

## When Am I Charged?

Card and UPI orders are authorized at checkout and captured when the order ships.

Cash on delivery is collected at your door.

Pre-orders are charged 24 hours before dispatch.

## Invoices and GST

A digital invoice is emailed within 1 hour of payment.

Business customers can add a GSTIN in Account Settings so it appears on every invoice.

Invoices can be re-sent by support at any time.

## Failed Payments

If money left your account but no order appeared, the payment processor auto-reverses it within 5 business days.

If it has been longer than 5 business days, contact support with the transaction reference and we will trace it.

## Promo Codes

Promo codes apply at checkout before payment.

Only one promo code can be used per order.

Codes have expiry dates and minimum spend requirements - the agent can validate any code for you before you shop.

Common issues:

- CODE10 requires a minimum spend of $50.
- WELCOME15 works once per customer on the first order.
- Expired codes cannot be revived, but ask the agent - active alternatives usually exist.

## Current Active Promotions

## Price Adjustments

If an item you bought drops in price within 7 days of delivery, we refund the difference as store credit. One adjustment per order.

## Store Credit

Store credit never expires and applies automatically at checkout.

It can be used together with a promo code.

## Chargebacks

We resolve most disputes faster than a bank can. Please contact support first - escalated billing cases go directly to our human lead.
