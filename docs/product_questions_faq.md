# Product Questions and Stock FAQ

How to check availability, product details and restocks before you buy.

## Checking Stock

The support agent can check live stock levels for any product by name or SKU.

Stock shown in chat reflects the warehouse snapshot at that moment; checkout performs the final reservation.

## Low Stock Alerts

Products with fewer than 5 units show a low stock warning on their page.

You can ask the agent to note your interest in a product; it will remember this preference for future visits.

## Restock Timelines

Most sold-out items restock within 2-4 weeks.

Seasonal items may not return until next season.

The agent can tell you the expected restock date where one is planned.

## Sizing Information

Apparel follows standard sizing charts published on each product page.

Shoes run true to size except the TrailRunner line, which runs half a size small.

## Product Care

Electronics: keep away from liquids unless the product advertises water resistance with an IP rating.

Leather goods: condition every 6 months.

Batteries: avoid full discharges; store at ~50% charge for long gaps.

## Compatibility Questions

Ask the agent about device compatibility - it can look up the knowledge base for known compatibility notes per product.

Common examples:

- AirSound Pro headphones work with any Bluetooth 5.0+ device.
- PowerHub 65W charges laptops that use USB-C PD only.

## Bulk Orders

Orders of 10+ identical units go through bulk sales for custom pricing.

Ask the agent to escalate a bulk inquiry and our human lead will respond within one business day.

## Product Recalls

Recalled products are identified by batch number in your order details.

If your product is recalled, stop using it immediately; support will arrange collection and a full refund without needing the return window.
