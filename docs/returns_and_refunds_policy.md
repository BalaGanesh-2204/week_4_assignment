# Returns and Refunds Policy

The complete rules for returning items and receiving refunds at ShopKart.

## Return Window

Most items can be returned within 30 days of the delivery date.

The day your order is marked delivered counts as day zero.

Items returned after the window are not eligible for a refund.

## Eligible Conditions

An item is eligible for return when:

- It is within the return window.
- It is in its original packaging with all accessories.
- It was not marked as final sale at purchase.

## Non-Returnable Items

The following cannot be returned:

- Items marked Final Sale at checkout.
- Gift cards.
- Perishable goods.
- Digital products that have been downloaded.
- Personalized or engraved items.

## Restocking Fee

A restocking fee of 10% of the item price applies only when ALL of these are true:

- The item is electronics (headphones, speakers, laptops, cameras).
- The box was opened.
- There is nothing wrong with the item (change-of-mind returns).

No restocking fee applies when the item is damaged, defective, incorrect, or missing parts. Those are always our fault and never your cost.

## Refund Amounts

Full refund = item price + original shipping for faulty items.

Change-of-mind returns refund the item price; original shipping is refunded only if the whole order is returned.

Refunds are calculated automatically by our support agent before any request is created, so you know the exact amount up front.

## Manager Review for Large Refunds

Refund requests of $200 or more are automatically routed to our human support lead (Balaganesh) for approval.

This protects customers from mistakes and protects the store from fraud. You will still get confirmation that your request was logged the moment it passes eligibility checks.

## Refund Timelines

Once approved:

- Credit/debit card: 5-7 business days.
- UPI/wallet: 1-2 business days.
- Store credit: instantly.

Your bank may take an extra billing cycle to display the credit.

## How to Start a Return

Just tell the support agent what happened and give your order ID. The agent will:

1. Check your order status and eligibility.
2. Calculate your exact refund amount.
3. Create the refund request.
4. Generate a prepaid return label.

## Exchanges

Exchanges follow the same rules as returns but ship the replacement as soon as the courier scans your return package, not when it arrives back at the warehouse.
