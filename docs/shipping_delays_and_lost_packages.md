# Shipping Delays and Lost Packages

Troubleshooting guide for orders that are late, stuck, or missing.

## Expected Delivery Windows

Delivery windows depend on your zone:

- Metro: 2-3 business days
- Urban: 3-5 business days
- Suburban: 4-6 business days
- Remote: 7-10 business days

Business days exclude weekends and public holidays.

## My Order Is Late. What Should I Do?

First, ask the support agent for the live status of your order using your order ID.

If the promised date has passed by more than 3 business days, we open a carrier investigation.

While the investigation runs you may choose:

- A replacement shipped immediately, or
- A full refund including original shipping.

## Tracking Has Not Updated for Days

Tracking updates can lag by up to 12 hours after each scan.

A shipment with no tracking movement for 7 consecutive days is treated as lost.

Lost packages are always replaced or refunded, and the case is reviewed by our human support lead before the replacement ships.

## Marked Delivered But Not Received

Check with neighbors and building reception first.

If still missing, contact support within 48 hours of the delivered scan. We will verify the GPS delivery point with the courier and reship or refund confirmed losses.

## Wrong Item Received

Keep the wrong item; we do not charge for return shipping in this case.

Contact support with your order ID. We dispatch the correct item immediately and send a prepaid label for the incorrect one.

## Partial Delivery

Multi-item orders sometimes arrive in separate boxes on different days.

Check your order detail page for per-item tracking before reporting a missing item.
