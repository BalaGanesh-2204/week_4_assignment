# Shipping Policy

ShopKart ships to all serviceable postal codes across the country. This page explains shipping zones, delivery timelines, costs, and tracking.

## Shipping Zones

Shipping zones are determined by the first digit of your postal code.

### Zone 1 - Metro (postal codes starting 1 or 2)

Standard delivery: 2-3 business days.

Express delivery: next business day.

Overnight delivery: delivered before 10:00 AM the next day.

### Zone 2 - Urban (postal codes starting 3 or 4)

Standard delivery: 3-5 business days.

Express delivery: 2 business days.

Overnight delivery: not available.

### Zone 3 - Suburban (postal codes starting 5 or 6)

Standard delivery: 4-6 business days.

Express delivery: 3 business days.

### Zone 4 - Remote (postal codes starting 7, 8 or 9)

Standard delivery: 7-10 business days.

Express delivery: 5 business days.

## Shipping Costs

| Method | Zone 1 | Zone 2 | Zone 3 | Zone 4 |
|--------|--------|--------|--------|--------|
| Standard | $4.99 | $5.99 | $7.99 | $9.99 |
| Express | $12.99 | $14.99 | $17.99 | $21.99 |
| Overnight | $24.99 | Not available | Not available | Not available |

Orders above $75 qualify for free standard shipping in all zones.

## Order Cut-off Times

Orders placed before 2:00 PM local time are dispatched the same business day.

Orders placed after 2:00 PM, on weekends, or on public holidays are dispatched the next business day.

## Tracking Your Order

Every order receives a tracking number once it is dispatched.

You can ask the support agent for the latest status of your order at any time using your order ID (for example ORD-1042).

Tracking updates may take up to 12 hours to appear after dispatch.

## Delayed or Lost Shipments

If a standard order has not arrived within 3 business days of the promised date, contact support.

Shipments that show no tracking movement for 7 consecutive days are treated as lost.

Lost shipments are replaced or refunded free of charge, and such cases are always reviewed by our human support lead.

## International Shipping

We currently do not ship outside the country.

Gift cards and digital products are delivered by email within 15 minutes of purchase and have no shipping fee.
