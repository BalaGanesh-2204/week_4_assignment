# Warranty and Repairs

What is covered, for how long, and how to get a product repaired.

## Warranty Tiers

### Standard Warranty - 12 Months

Applies to all electronics: headphones, speakers, laptops, cameras, smartwatches.

Covers manufacturing defects, battery failure above normal wear, and hardware faults under normal use.

### Extended Warranty - 24 Months

Available for purchase within 30 days of the original order.

Costs 10% of the item price and adds 12 more months of the same coverage.

### Accessories - 6 Months

Cables, cases, chargers and straps carry a 6-month warranty against defects.

## What Is NOT Covered

- Physical damage caused after delivery (drops, water damage beyond IP rating, pet damage).
- Normal wear such as ear-pad foam or scratched finishes.
- Products bought from other sellers or marketplaces.
- Items with tampered serial numbers.

## How to Claim

Tell the support agent:

1. Your order ID.
2. What went wrong.

The agent checks warranty eligibility automatically and either creates a repair ticket or routes you to a replacement/refund if the fault qualifies.

## Repair Process

Approved repairs are handled at authorized service centers.

Turnaround is typically 7-10 business days plus shipping time.

You get SMS updates when the unit is received, diagnosed, repaired and dispatched.

## Replacement vs Repair

Within the first 7 days after delivery, confirmed defects are replaced directly, not repaired.

After 7 days, the service center decides between repair and replacement based on the diagnosis.

Repeated failures of the same component (2 or more repairs) qualify you to request an upgrade to a replacement instead of another repair. Such cases are reviewed by our support lead.

## Out-of-Warranty Repairs

Out-of-warranty units can still be repaired for a diagnostic fee of $25 plus parts.

The fee is waived if you accept the repair quote.

## Proof of Purchase

Your order ID is your proof of purchase - no separate invoice needed for warranty claims through ShopKart.
